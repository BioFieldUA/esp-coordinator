⚠️ Flash Mode Activation:
  1. Connect the ESP32-board to your computer via USB.
  2. Press and hold the BOOT button.
  3. Click the RESET button once.
  4. Release the BOOT button.
  5. Now click the Connect button below to start flashing.
  6. After successful flashing click the RESET button once again to launch your Coordinator.
